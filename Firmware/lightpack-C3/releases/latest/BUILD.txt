0.6.3 adds network provisioning over USB. The console's Shared -> Network panel
sends @WIFI {"ssid","pass"}; the board stores slot 0, leaves offline mode and
starts the join, using the same path as POST /api/wifi. No 6 s hotspot hold is
needed. The password is never read back.

Update works on a board running 0.5.0-dev or newer. Recovery uses the vendored
esptool-js 0.7.1 through the C3 ROM: hold GPIO9 (BOOT) at reset, release when the
port appears, then click Recovery. Recovery writes boot_app0 at 0xE000 and the app
at 0x10000, leaving NVS (settings, presets, networks) untouched.
