0.6 adds the power budget and 10 Hz guard, the rainbow level, the gyro-fade
tunables and preset v3, together with the bench stress/sense tools and safe mode.
During a USB OTA the strip is held off and the status pixel heartbeats purple;
a stalled update shows solid purple and a failed one gives three red blinks.
A board on 0.5.0-dev or newer can update from the console.
