0.6.2 adds the console Recovery button. It uses the vendored esptool-js 0.7.1
through the C3 ROM bootloader: hold GPIO9 (BOOT) at reset, release when the port
appears, then click Recovery. It writes boot_app0.bin at 0xE000 and the app at
0x10000; NVS is preserved. This is the path for boards that predate @UPDATE.
The device page keeps the single Update button as the fallback.
