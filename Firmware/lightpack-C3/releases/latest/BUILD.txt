The USB updater is the app-level @UPDATE path (0.5.0-dev and newer):
tools/usb_updater.py or the console page. The strip is held off, the status pixel
heartbeats purple, goes solid on a stall and blinks red three times on failure.
Old boards without @UPDATE use tools/usb_installer.py: hold GPIO9 (BOOT) at reset
to enter the ROM install mode; it writes boot_app0.bin at 0xE000 and the app at
0x10000, leaving NVS untouched.
